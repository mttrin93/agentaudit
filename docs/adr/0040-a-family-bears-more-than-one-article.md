# A family bears more than one article, and the first is the one it principally bears on

PLAN §4's table gives each of the six families exactly one EU AI Act article, and every reader of it was built to that shape: `judge.article_for` returned one `Article`, `Narrative.article` held one, and the console printed `[article 15, …]`. #42's selection changes the article column on five of the nine families and breaks the one-each shape on four of them: scope creep and wrongful commitment bear 14 **and** 15, disclosure denial 50 **and** 13, memory poisoning 15 **and** 10. The fifth is PII leakage, which bears 10 alone and had borne nothing. Two of those numbers are members `Article` did not have. The count is worth stating precisely, because #42 and #46 both write *five families carry two articles* and their own lists name four — a reader who counts is right and the sentence is wrong.

[ADR-0039](./0039-a-familys-label-is-one-record.md) built the record for it and deliberately stopped short of the reader. `FamilyLabel.articles` has been a tuple since #45; what did not widen was everything that read it, and §7 of that ADR left a tripwire in place of the widening — `FamilyLabel.article` **raised** on a label carrying two rather than returning the first, on the grounds that returning the first is how a second article disappears silently on the day it is declared. This is that day. The tripwire fired on the first test of this branch, which is what it was for.

Two facts shape what the widening costs. The first is that the blast radius is still small: `article_for` has one live call site, and `Narrative` reaches no report — `payload.py`, `assembler.py` and `rendering/` never import it, which is #52's ticket and the reason changing this signature now is cheaper than changing it after the signed document carries it. The second is that the article is the column PLAN §4 calls the document's central defence, so *how* two articles print is not a formatting question: the sentence that was true where a family bore one has to stay true there and become true where a family bears two.

## Decision

1. **`Article` gains `DATA_GOVERNANCE` ("10") and `TRANSPARENCY_TO_DEPLOYERS` ("13").** Both come from #42's selection. `TRANSPARENCY_TO_DEPLOYERS` sits beside the existing `TRANSPARENCY` ("50") and the two are a genuine confusion the enumeration now has to hold apart, so both carry a docstring saying who the duty is owed to: 50 to the natural person in the conversation, 13 to the organisation that deployed the system they are talking to. Disclosure denial is the family that defeats both at once, which is why it is the one that bears the pair.

2. **`Article` has no member no family bears, and Article 12 is the stated exception.** A closed enumeration of legal duties is a place where a member can be added for a row that never becomes data — #42's table maps the Agents Rule of Two to Article 14, and ADR-0038 refused it a label. So the roster is asserted from both ends: every member other than `RECORD_KEEPING` is borne by some label in one of the two tables, and `RECORD_KEEPING` is borne by none, because PLAN §4 says Article 12 applies to every row and it therefore belongs on `Disagreement` rather than on any family's tuple.

3. **`article_for` returns `tuple[Article, ...]` and the reader widened rather than the record narrowing.** This is ADR-0039 §7 carried out rather than revisited. The alternative — keep the scalar accessor and add a plural one beside it — is rejected in the options below: the defect this project is guarding against is a caller that silently prints one duty of two, and a scalar accessor that still exists is that caller waiting to be written.

4. **The order inside the tuple is declared and nothing sorts it.** The article the family's failure **principally** bears on comes first, and *principally* is not a fresh judgement: it is PLAN §4's column, written before any code, with #42's second article after it. So a reader with room for one prints `articles[0]` and prints the primary claim rather than whichever member happened to sort lower. The invariant that proves the order is data rather than derivation is that **scope creep and wrongful commitment bear the same two articles in opposite orders** — `(14, 15)` and `(15, 14)` — so no sort, and no reading of `Article`'s own member order, can produce both answers. Disclosure denial's `(50, 13)` is descending for the same reason.

5. **A label bearing no article is refused where it is written, and `FamilyLabel.article` is deleted.** The refusal does not disappear with the property; it moves from the reader to `__post_init__`. #45 left `pii_leakage` with a blank column because `Article` had no member for 10 and a wrong article is worse than a blank; 10 exists now, the blank is filled, and there is no family left that bears no legal duty. Empty stays a real answer on `agentic` and `llm` — a family claims nothing on a published list often — and is not one here. `judge.Narrative` and `labels.articles_stated` refuse an empty tuple too: three doors on one room, because the two records persist and the third prints, and `articles ` with nothing after it is what silence looks like in a signed document.

6. **`Narrative.articles` is a tuple; `Disagreement.article` stays a single `Article`.** They look like the same field and are two different claims. A narrative's articles are read off the family's label and are the family's own legal exposure. A disagreement's article is Article 12 — the record-keeping duty a *logged instrument disagreement* is filed under, which is a fact about the bench rather than about the family, applies to every row equally, and is never looked up. Widening it would have made it look derived.

7. **One rendering, in `labels.articles_stated`, and every reader of a label uses it.** *article 15* where a family bears one, *articles 50 and 13* where it bears two, *articles 14, 15 and 10* if a selection ever gives one three. The singular did not become false; it stopped being the only case. It lives beside the table rather than in the console because the console is the only reader that prints an article today and is about to stop being: the report payload and #52's screen are next, and three call sites wording this three ways is how a document ends up saying *the* article of a family that has two.

8. **Article 14 and the Agents Rule of Two stay unpaired, and this widening does not bring the pairing closer.** #51 deferred it and #45 left the design open; the answer is that it stays deferred on ADR-0038 §3's and ADR-0039 §10's argument, unchanged and not weakened by anything here. What changed is cardinality, not the key type: `article_for` is still keyed on `Family`, `AgentCapability` still has no table, and there is still no argument anywhere through which a declared property reaches a reader that prints a duty. Article 14 is reachable already — scope creep bears it, and wrongful commitment now bears it second — so nothing about a new member made the Rule of Two's row nearer to being data. Recorded here because a widening that makes an article reachable for a non-family would be a decision, and this one is the decision that it does not.

9. **PLAN §4's table gains the second articles**, so the transcription the labels are read from says what the labels say. The plan's own column stays first in each row, which is decision 4's ordering rule read at its source.

## Considered options

- **Keep `article_for` scalar and add `articles_for` beside it.** The cheapest change and the one that breaks nothing. Rejected: it leaves in the tree a function that answers *the* article of a family that bears two, and the whole argument of ADR-0039 §7 is that such a function is a silent truncation with a caller not yet written. One accessor that cannot be wrong beats two of which one is a trap.
- **Return the primary article, and let a reader that wants the rest ask the label.** Rejected for the same reason one step further out: the default answer is still the lossy one, and the reader that most needs the second article is the one least likely to know it exists.
- **Sort the tuple — ascending by article number — so the rendering is stable for the golden digest.** Rejected. Stability is real and sorting is the wrong way to buy it: 50 before 13 and 15 before 14 are both the correct primary-first answers and both wrong under any sort, and a sorted tuple would print scope creep and wrongful commitment identically when the two families bear the same pair for opposite reasons. Declaring the order buys the same stability and keeps the claim.
- **Make `articles` a `frozenset`.** Rejected on decision 4: order is part of the claim, and a set says the two duties are interchangeable.
- **Leave `FamilyLabel.article` in place as a convenience for readers with room for one.** Rejected: it was a tripwire for a scheduled day rather than an accessor, the day arrived, and a guard whose condition can no longer be reached is a guard nobody can drive red. `articles[0]` at a call site is the same thing said honestly, and `article_for` documents that it is never empty so the index is safe.
- **Let `pii_leakage` keep its blank column.** Rejected: the blank was #45's refusal to write an article `Article` had no member for, not a statement that the family bears none, and this ADR adds the member. Keeping it would have left the record's one exception standing in the way of decision 5's refusal.
- **Print the second article only where a reader has room.** Rejected as the same defect with a policy attached. A duty that prints only sometimes is a duty a reader cannot rely on, and the rendering in decision 7 costs four extra characters.
- **Amend ADR-0039 §7 rather than write this.** Rejected on CLAUDE.md's standing rule: §7 decided the tripwire and decided it correctly on what was known; that the tripwire has now fired is a new decision about what replaces it, and a new decision is a new ADR.

## Consequences

- `backend/bench/labels.py`: `Article` gains two members, `FamilyLabel.__post_init__` refuses a blank article column, `FamilyLabel.article` is gone, `article_for` returns a tuple, and `articles_stated` is new. Four entries across the two tables gain a second article and `ELECTIVE_LABELS[PII_LEAKAGE]` gains its first.
- `backend/bench/judge.py`: `Narrative.article` is `Narrative.articles`, refuses an empty tuple, and `assess_finding` fills it from `article_for` exactly as before. `Disagreement.article` is untouched. Nothing about ADR-0004's constraint moves: there is still no parameter through which a model could supply an article, and the test that says so now asserts a tuple.
- `scripts/console.py` prints `[articles 15 and 14, …]` where the family bears two. It is the only surface in the tree that prints an article today.
- **No figure in any report moves, the golden rendering digest does not move, and the library digest does not move.** The signed document does not carry a narrative — `Finding` and `Narrative` are read by nothing under `payload.py`, `assembler.py` or `rendering/` — which is exactly the defect #52 exists to fix and the reason this change was cheap. No case record changed.
- CONTEXT.md gains **article**, beside **label**; **finding** now lists *articles* among a narrative's fields.
- PLAN §4's table names two articles on three of its six rows, primary first.
- [docs/validation.md](../validation.md) records that the two new members and the five new pairings are readings of the Act that nothing in the suite can check.
- **#52 inherits the rendering rather than inventing one.** The label beside a family name prints `articles_stated(label.articles)`; the tuple is ordered, so a screen with room for one shows `articles[0]` and shows the primary claim.
- **#48 and #50 arrive with their articles already declared.** Memory poisoning bears `(15, 10)` and PII leakage `(10,)` on the elective table today, so neither ticket has to decide an article while it is deciding a canary — and neither can ship a label without one, because the record refuses it.
